More Than a Café

Every cup and every sandwich helps fund something bigger.

A Seat for Everyone

Living Free Cafe is a warm, judgment-free spot in Pahrump for good coffee, an affordable meal, and a place to sit for a while.

Real Work, New Skills

Behind the counter, the café is a working classroom — real job experience, confidence, and new career skills in an understanding workplace.

Proceeds With a Purpose

Every purchase supports Living Free Health's addiction treatment, sober transitional living, and community programs.
